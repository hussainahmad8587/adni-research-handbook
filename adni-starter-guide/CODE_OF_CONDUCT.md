# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a welcoming project for researchers at every level. Be respectful, assume good faith, give specific and kind feedback, and keep discussion on the content.

Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may block users who violate these expectations.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers (see the repository's Security/Contact settings). This text is a short placeholder; consider adopting the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) in full.
