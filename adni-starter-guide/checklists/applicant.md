# First-time applicant checklist

- [ ] I have a short question, the data types I need, and a plan for analysis.
- [ ] I reviewed the [official access form](https://adni.loni.usc.edu/data-samples/adni-data/#AccessData) and the [Data Use Agreement](https://adni.loni.usc.edu/wp-content/themes/adni_2023/documents/ADNI_Data_Use_Agreement.pdf).
- [ ] I checked who the form asks me to list and how access applies to my team.
- [ ] I checked institutional ethics and storage requirements.
- [ ] My approved storage is outside this repository.
- [ ] After approval, I can open IDA and record exactly which exports I retrieved.
