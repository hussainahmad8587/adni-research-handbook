**By the end:** You’ll have a short research plan and know where to submit it.

## 1. Prepare your application

Read the [ADNI Data Use Agreement](https://adni.loni.usc.edu/wp-content/themes/adni_2023/documents/ADNI_Data_Use_Agreement.pdf). Draft a short plan that explains your research question, which data you need, and how you will analyze them. Check the current application for investigator and team details, and ask your institution about any ethics and storage requirements.

**Example research plan**

> We will estimate how many eligible participants in selected ADNI phases have a structural MRI near their baseline visit. We will link visit and scan records, count unique participants, and report the results by phase.

## 2. Submit your request

Use the [official ADNI data access page](https://adni.loni.usc.edu/data-samples/adni-data/#AccessData) and follow the instructions in the application. If your project requires physical specimens, such as blood or DNA samples, use the separate [biospecimen access process](https://adni.loni.usc.edu/data-samples/adni-samples/#ApplyForAccessToSamples).

## 3. After approval

Sign in to the [LONI Image and Data Archive](https://ida.loni.usc.edu/) with your approved account. Start with the files needed for your research question, and record what you download and when.

Keep ADNI data in institution-approved storage. Review the agreement’s requirements for team access, data use, and annual updates. Keep participant-level records, scans, and notebook outputs out of public repositories.
